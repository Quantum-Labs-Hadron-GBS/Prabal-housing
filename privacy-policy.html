Privacy Policy

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. | Owned and operated by PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd.

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. has created this privacy statement in order to demonstrate our commitment to our users’ privacy.

This Privacy Policy describes the manner in which PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. collects, holds, and uses personal information. If you wish to make any inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, you should contact PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd.’s Privacy Officer by email at info@prabalhousing.com.

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. may, from time to time, review and update this Privacy Policy to take account of new laws and technology. All personal information held by PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. will be governed by its most recent policy, posted on this website.

1. What is “Personal Information”?

“Personal Information” is any information or opinion about a person, whether true or not, from which that person may be identified.

"Personal information" means any information that relates to a natural person, which, either directly or indirectly, in combination with other information available or likely to be available with a body corporate, is capable of identifying such person. Defined under Section 2(i) Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

2. When do we collect Personal Information?

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. collects Personal Information from its website and App users and customers in a variety of ways. PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. may collect Personal Information such as your name, email address, contact details, and country/state location when you visit or register with our website and Mobile App or contact us by telephone or email.

Information collected via this website

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. will collect Personal Information about visitors to this website when they knowingly provide it or as otherwise described below. For example, PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. will collect Personal Information from users of its website when they inquire on the PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. website (as a buyer or seller) or add to their details on selected properties.

When you visit and browse this website, the PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. website host will collect Personal Information for statistical, marketing, reporting and maintenance purposes.

This information will be used to identify you and may include:

  • The number of users visiting this website or a property listing and the number of pages viewed.
  • The date, time and duration of a visit.
  • Your physical location.
  • The path taken through this website.

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd.’s website host uses this information to administer and improve the performance of this website and to provide de-identified generic data to people who have listed their property on this website.

Cookies are small text files that are transferred to a user’s computer hard drive by a website for the purpose of storing information about a user’s identity, browser type or website visiting patterns.

If you access this website, a cookie may be downloaded onto your computer’s hard drive when you first log onto this website and, if it is, it will be automatically deleted when you log out or close your web browser.

Web beacons are images that originate from a third-party site to track visitor activities. PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. may use web beacons to track the visiting patterns of individuals accessing this website.

The Company shall collect and process such information only for specified, lawful and legitimate business purposes, subject to applicable laws and the terms of this Privacy Policy

3. How PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. uses the Personal Information it collects about you

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. uses the Personal Information it collects about you to:

  • Provide you with products or services you have requested.
  • Personalise and customise your experiences with this website.
  • Help PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. research the needs of its users and market its products and services with a better understanding of your needs and the needs of users generally.
  • Communicate with you.
  • Provide de-identified data about PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. customers to advertisers or merchants to assist them in providing the most relevant products to website users.
  • Provide de-identified data about PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. customers to advertisers and merchants to sell advertising space on the PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. website.
  • Provide de-identified data about a property listing to the individual who listed the property on this website.
  • Use other Personal Information provided voluntarily by you to better understand PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. users and to help improve and promote its services.
  • Collect and use your Personal Information only in accordance with this Privacy Policy or as notified to you at the point of collection.

If you contact PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. by email for assistance with or information on the operation of the website, you authorise PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. to contact you by telephone (if you have provided this) in order to service your enquiry/problem.

If you choose not to provide your Personal Information to PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, the only consequence for you will be that we will not be able to provide you with the requested service.

The Company may process Personal Information for the purposes of managing property enquiries, facilitating communication between buyers, sellers, developers and real estate agents, providing property-related information, coordinating site visits, processing customer requests, maintaining business records, preventing fraudulent activities, resolving disputes and complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

The Company may also use such information for internal analytics, service improvement and business development purposes, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

To whom we disclose your Personal Information

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. discloses de-identified information about its website users, customers and potential customers to current and potential advertisers and merchants on the PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. website to enable them to connect users with relevant products that match users’ needs and wants.

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. may disclose your Personal Information to our website host in certain limited circumstances, for example when the website experiences a technical problem, to ensure that it operates effectively and securely.

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. may disclose your Personal Information to third parties as required for your use of the website or any of its services. For example, information may be disclosed to a vendor or their agent if you submit a query regarding a property, or to real estate agents if you use our services.

The Company may share Personal Information with developers, builders, property owners, real estate agents, brokers, service providers, technology partners and other relevant third parties, strictly to the extent necessary for responding to user enquiries, facilitating property transactions, providing requested services or fulfilling legitimate business requirements, subject to applicable law.

If you choose to become a customer of the PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. website, the information accessible to other website users is limited to the following:

  • Your name
  • The address of the property listed by you for sale
  • Your property photo(s) (if provided by you)
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number (if provided by you)

4. Marketing

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. may use your Personal Information to provide you with promotional material about PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. or status reports on your property or on any of the services offered by PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd., whether you are a seller, a buyer or a member. If you do not wish to receive this type of promotional material, you can contact PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd.’s Privacy Officer by email at info@prabalhousing.com so that we can remove your name from our marketing lists. If you choose not to receive this type of promotional material, you may limit some of the services you receive from PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd..

Otherwise, PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. will not disclose any of your Personal Information to any other organisation unless the disclosure is required by law or is otherwise permitted by the National Privacy Principles.

5. Updating, storage and security of Personal Information held by PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd.

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. aims to keep your Personal Information secure and up to date. Any Personal Information or financial information collected via this website or held on PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd.’s computer systems is protected by safeguards including physical, technical and procedural methods.

PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. does not collect sensitive information about its users via its website.

Personal Information and financial information held by PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. in hard copy is stored securely on its premises and is only disclosed or used for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

You can update your contact and profile information at any time by contacting our Privacy Officer. If we find that we have no further need for your Personal Information or financial information, we will remove it from our system and destroy all records of it. PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. welcomes any changes to your details so as to keep our records up to date.

The Company shall retain Personal Information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, or for such additional period as may be required under applicable laws, regulatory requirements, contractual obligations, dispute resolution, prevention of fraud or establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

Upon expiry of the applicable retention period, the Company shall securely delete, destroy or anonymise such information in accordance with its data retention and disposal procedures, subject to applicable law.

6. Privacy Disclaimer

This Privacy Policy is subject to the relevant legislation set out above. To the extent that this Policy provides for greater privacy protection than required by law, PrabalHousing Pvt. Ltd. will not be liable for any loss, liability, cost, expenses or damage arising as a result of it failing to meet that increased standard of privacy protection under this Privacy Policy.
