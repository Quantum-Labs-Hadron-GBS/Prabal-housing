Careers FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What career opportunities and roles are available at PRABALHOUSING?

PRABALHOUSING actively hires across multiple real estate advisory domains in Pune, including Luxury Real Estate Advisors, Business Development Managers, Portfolio Consultants, and Digital Marketing Specialists. We welcome both seasoned property consultants and high-potential sales professionals who are eager to master the luxury residential and commercial property markets.

What does the recruitment and interview process look like at PRABALHOUSING?

Our hiring process is transparent, structured, and fast—typically concluding within 5 to 7 business days. It consists of three stages: (1) Initial Application and Profile Review by our talent acquisition team, (2) A Competency and Culture Conversation assessing communication skills, consultative mindset, and career goals, and (3) A Final Discussion with Senior Leadership to discuss compensation, incentive tiers, and growth paths.

Does PRABALHOUSING hire freshers or candidates without prior real estate experience?

Yes. PRABALHOUSING welcomes driven fresh graduates as well as professionals transitioning from banking, wealth management, hospitality, corporate sales, or customer service. Every recruit undergoes a comprehensive 4-week PRABAL Advisory Training Bootcamp covering Pune micro-markets, MahaRERA legal frameworks, architectural literacy, and high-trust consultative client engagement.

What compensation, performance incentives, and benefits are offered?

We offer competitive fixed monthly compensation coupled with uncapped, performance-driven transaction incentives that represent some of the highest earning multipliers in the Pune luxury real estate sector. Team members also receive comprehensive medical coverage, mobile and conveyance allowances, sponsored sales certifications, and rapid merit-based promotion tracks to leadership roles.

Where is the PRABALHOUSING office located and what are the working hours?

Our headquarters is centrally located at 7th Floor, Pyramid Axis, Baner, Pune, Maharashtra 411045. Standard office hours are Monday through Saturday from 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM with rotational weekly offs. Client property inspections are supported with company executive travel arrangements.
