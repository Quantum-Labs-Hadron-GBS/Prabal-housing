Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

You charge 0% brokerage. How do you earn money?

The builder pays us a fee when a sale is completed. This comes from the builder's own marketing budget. It is not added to your price, and you never pay us anything.

Will I pay more than if I go to the builder directly?

No. You get the same price or a better one. Because we bring many serious buyers to builders, we can often get you extra offers, upgrades or easier payment plans.

How do I know the projects are safe and genuine?

We only work with RERA-registered projects from well-known builders. Before you decide, we explain the approvals, the property papers and the good and bad points of each option.

If I contact you, do I have to buy?

No. Getting options and visiting projects is free, and you are never forced to buy. No spam and no pressure. We only call you when you want us to.

Which parts of Pune do you cover?

All the main residential areas, including Baner, Wakad, Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Hadapsar, Wagholi and Kothrud. If you are not sure where to buy, we will help you decide.
