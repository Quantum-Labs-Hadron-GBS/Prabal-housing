Good questions

WHAT'S THE CATCH?

If I pay 0% brokerage, how do you make money?

Developers pay us a standard channel-partner fee when a sale closes. It comes out of their marketing budget — it isn't added to your price, and you never pay us anything.

Will I pay more than booking directly with the developer?

No. You get the same or better price than walking in yourself — and because we bring developers a steady stream of serious buyers, we can often negotiate offers, upgrades or payment plans you wouldn't get alone.

How do I know the projects are genuine?

We only work with RERA-registered projects from established developers. Before you commit, we walk you through the approvals, title documents and the honest pros & cons of each option.

Am I obligated to buy if I enquire?

Not at all. Getting a shortlist and visiting projects is free and carries zero obligation. No spam, no pressure — we only call when you want us to.

Which parts of Pune do you cover?

All the major residential micro-markets — Baner, Wakad, Hinjewadi, Kharadi, Hadapsar, Wagholi, Kothrud and more. If you're not sure where to buy, we'll help you decide.
