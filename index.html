Questions & Answers

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

You charge 0% brokerage. How do you earn money?

PRABALHOUSING operates as an authorized institutional channel partner for Pune's premier real estate developers. When you purchase a property through us, developers pay our advisory fee directly from their dedicated marketing budget. This fee is never added to your property purchase price—in fact, our institutional volume allows us to negotiate exclusive pricing, waivers, and flexible payment terms that individual walk-in buyers rarely receive. Homebuyers never pay PRABALHOUSING any consultation fees, brokerage fees, or administrative charges at any stage.

Will I pay more than if I go to the builder directly?

No, you will never pay more than going directly to the builder—in almost all cases, you pay less. Because PRABALHOUSING brings significant transaction volume to top developers across Baner, Wakad, Kharadi, and Hinjewadi, we hold institutional negotiating leverage. We routinely secure corporate price discounts, complimentary interior upgrades, preferred floor selections, and flexible subvention or staggered payment schedules that direct walk-in buyers cannot access on their own.

How do I know the projects are safe, legal, and RERA-approved?

Every single property represented by PRABALHOUSING is 100% MahaRERA-approved and registered with legitimate RERA registration numbers from verified, reputed developers in Pune. We do not list or recommend unapproved projects or unauthorized land under any circumstances. Furthermore, our legal and architectural evaluation team audits land title deeds, environmental clearances, layout sanctions, commencement certificates (CC), and the developer's delivery track record. You receive an objective, verified legal dossier before you visit or make any financial commitment.

If I contact you, do I have to buy? Do you provide site visit cab service?

Absolutely not. PRABALHOUSING is founded on an advisory-first philosophy with zero sales pressure. Getting curated shortlists, financial comparisons, market appreciation reports, and guided site visits is 100% complimentary with zero purchase obligation. Furthermore, we provide a 100% free private AC cab and chauffeur service to pick you up directly from your doorstep anywhere in Pune, accompany you to inspect shortlisted RERA-approved properties in complete comfort, and drop you back safely—all at zero expense to you.

Do you provide free cab service for site visits across Pune?

Yes! PRABALHOUSING provides a 100% complimentary private AC cab service with chauffeur pickup and drop directly from your home or office anywhere in Pune. Our dedicated property advisor accompanies you in a private, sanitized vehicle to tour multiple shortlisted RERA-approved projects in a single, stress-free visit. You never pay for fuel, cab booking, or advisory services.

Which parts of Pune do you cover?

We provide comprehensive coverage across Pune's top residential and investment corridors. In West Pune, we cover prime growth hubs including Baner, Balewadi, Wakad, Hinjewadi (Phase 1, 2, 3), Punawale, Ravet, and Bavdhan. In East Pune, we specialize in high-demand IT and lifestyle clusters such as Kharadi, Viman Nagar, Kalyani Nagar, Magarpatta, Hadapsar, and Wagholi. We also advise on central and heritage locations like Kothrud, Senapati Bapat Road, Model Colony, and Aundh—covering only RERA-registered projects.

Do you assist with home loans, legal documentation, and property registration in Pune?

Yes, PRABALHOUSING provides complete end-to-end assistance from initial property search to final key handover. In addition to doorstep free cab site visits, our dedicated post-sales and banking desk assists with competitive home loan sanctioning across leading nationalized and private banks (such as SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank) at the lowest eligible interest rates. We also coordinate title search certificates, draft sales agreements, handle stamp duty and registration appointments at the sub-registrar office, and guide you through tax benefits.

How is PRABALHOUSING different from traditional real estate brokers in Pune?

Unlike traditional local brokers who charge a 1% to 2% brokerage commission from buyers and frequently push specific unverified inventory for high margins, PRABALHOUSING operates on a 100% zero-brokerage advisory model exclusively with RERA-approved developments. We provide free private AC cab site visits, institutional-grade market research, unbiased developer comparisons across all leading builders, RERA verification, transparent pricing, and comprehensive legal and financial advisory—all at zero cost to the homebuyer.
